About Adkrak Innovations

We build software for
the industries that never sleep.

Three engineers, one belief — the software that runs restaurants, schools and highways should feel as good as the apps we all use every day. So we're rebuilding it, category by category.

Our story

We came from the industries we now serve.

Every one of us has watched a restaurant owner curse a broken POS in the middle of a Saturday night rush. Watched a principal stay up till midnight generating report cards on a spreadsheet. Watched a traveller stranded on a highway at 2 AM with no idea who to call.

The tools for these people have not kept up. They're still using software that looks like it was designed in 2008 — because it was. Enterprise vendors sell them 40-module bloatware they never use, at prices no small operator can justify.

We think there's a better way. Build clean, opinionated software that solves the specific pain the operator actually has. Ship it fast. Price it fairly. Iterate with real customers, not with slide decks.

Adkrak Innovations exists to do exactly that — one category at a time. Restaurants first. Then schools. Then roads. Then everything else that makes real life happen.
