FAQ

Questions, answered.

What hardware do I need for bus tracking? +

Any standard 4G GPS tracker (we recommend specific tested models) plus the driver app on a low-cost Android phone. We can supply or you can source locally. Setup is half a day per bus.

How does the AI proctoring work? +

During an online exam, the student's webcam and screen are monitored. Our AI flags suspicious moments (tab-switch, multiple faces, talking, looking away). The teacher reviews only the flagged 5-10 seconds — not the whole hour.

Will it work on basic Android phones? +

Yes. Built specifically for low-RAM Android phones. Offline-first mode means rural-area teachers can keep marking attendance and entering grades even without signal.

How long to migrate from our current ERP? +

2–4 weeks for a full migration with data import, training, parallel-run, go-live. We've moved schools from Campus Care, MyClassboard, Edsys, Fedena, and others.

Is it NEP 2020 / CBSE / NAAC compliant? +

Yes. Holistic progress cards, competency-based assessment, vocational tracks, bag-less day reports, NAAC SSR fields, NIRF format exports — all built in.

Who owns the data? +

You do. Hosted in Indian data centres. Full export available any time. We follow DPDPA (India's data protection law) by design. On-premise option available for trusts that require it.
