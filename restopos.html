FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need new hardware? +

No. RestoPOS runs on any Android tablet or Windows PC you already own. Thermal printers and barcode scanners we'll source for you at cost.

What happens if the internet goes down? +

RestoPOS keeps running fully offline. Bills print, KOTs go out, data syncs to the cloud automatically the moment you're online again.

How long does setup take? +

From signup to first bill: under 24 hours. We upload your menu, configure tax, train your staff over a video call, and stay on standby for the first dinner service.

Can I migrate from another POS? +

Yes. We import menus and customer data from Petpooja, PosBytz, Posist and most CSV/Excel exports — free of charge.

Is my data safe? +

Daily encrypted backups, ISO-grade Indian data centres, role-based access. You can export your full data anytime — zero lock-in.
